      How this page is filtered

      What gets in

      • Only the approved sources listed here.
      • Only links to that source's own website.
      • Recent stories only, with near-identical headlines shown once.

      What stays out

      • Opinion, sport, entertainment and shopping.
      • Market tips and buy or sell suggestions.
      • Video, podcasts and routine notices.

      Known limits

      • Filtering uses keyword and web address rules, so some off-topic or weak stories can get through.
      • Stock indexes such as the S&P 500 are not shown, because republishing their data needs permission.
      • Reuters, AP, BBC, The Guardian and DW are not included. Some other sources may carry AP reports.
      • This page is general information, not financial advice.
